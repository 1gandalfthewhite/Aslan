Privacy Policy

PokeMy · Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the PokeMy mobile application, provided by Kılıç Aslan Doğan (“we”, “us”, “our”), a mobile app company based in Ankara, Türkiye. It describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you use PokeMy.

1. Information we collect

The only personal data we store about you is your username. We use it so you can create second-hand sale listings and message other users. We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or any other personal data.

To provide these features, we also store:

  • Sale listings you create (e.g. card details, photos, price, and description), shown publicly with your username
  • Messages you send to other users, so they can be delivered and shown in your conversations

2. Your collection stays on your device

The Pokémon collection you save in PokeMy is stored locally on your phone. We do not upload or store your collection on our servers. If you delete the app, your locally saved collection may be lost.

3. How we use your information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Show your listings to other users under your username
  • Deliver messages between users
  • Show current market values for cards in your collection
  • Keep the app safe, handle reports of abuse, and comply with legal obligations

We do not use your data for advertising and we do not build profiles about you.

4. Legal basis (KVKK)

Where applicable under Turkish law, including the Law on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK, Law No. 6698), we process personal data based on consent, contract performance, legitimate interests, or legal obligation, as appropriate.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. Your username and listings are visible to other PokeMy users, and your messages are visible to the users you message. We never share your data with third parties, except where we are required to do so by law.

6. Data retention

We keep your username, listings, and messages only as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You can delete your listings at any time. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your username and the related listings and messages, unless we are required by law to keep them.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your data, to data portability, and to object or withdraw consent. To delete your account and username, or to exercise these rights, contact us.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

9. Children

PokeMy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the latest version. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at aslansrkt@gmail.com or Kılıç Aslan Doğan, Ankara, Türkiye.
