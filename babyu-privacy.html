Privacy Policy

BabyU · Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the BabyU mobile application, provided by Kılıç Aslan Doğan (“we”, “us”, “our”), a mobile app company based in Ankara, Türkiye. It describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you use BabyU.

1. Information we collect

We may collect:

  • Ultrasound images that you choose to upload in the app
  • Contact and identity information (e.g. name, email) when you get in touch with us
  • Usage data (e.g. how you use the app and device type)

2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process the ultrasound image you upload and deliver the result to you
  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services and mobile apps
  • Respond to your requests and communicate with you
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights

We do not use your ultrasound images to train AI models, and we do not use them for advertising or any purpose other than generating your result.

3. Legal basis (KVKK)

Where applicable under Turkish law, including the Law on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK, Law No. 6698), we process personal data based on consent, contract performance, legitimate interests, or legal obligation, as appropriate. Because ultrasound images may be considered health-related data, we process them only with your explicit consent, given when you choose to upload an image.

4. How your ultrasound images are processed

Ultrasound images you upload are sent over an encrypted connection to our own servers. They are processed solely on our own machines; we do not send them to any third-party AI service or processor. As soon as the result has been delivered to your device, the uploaded image and all data generated during processing are immediately and permanently deleted from our servers.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, or share your ultrasound images, results, or other personal data with third parties. We never share your data with third parties, except where we are required to do so by law.

6. Data retention

Ultrasound images and processing results are not stored: they are deleted immediately after the result is delivered to you. Any result saved in the app stays on your own device under your control. Other data is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your data, to data portability, and to object or withdraw consent. Contact us to exercise these rights.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse, including encrypted transfer of uploaded images and immediate deletion after processing.

9. Children

BabyU is intended for adults (expecting parents and their families). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the latest version. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at aslansrkt@gmail.com or Kılıç Aslan Doğan, Ankara, Türkiye.
